# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

We want a harassment free experience for everyone. Be respectful, assume good
intent, and keep discussion technical and constructive.

PeregrineStoop is for authorized security testing only. Do not use the project,
its issues, or its discussions to coordinate or request testing against systems
you are not authorized to test.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer: haind@ptit.edu.vn.
